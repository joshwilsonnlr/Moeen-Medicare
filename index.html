Welcome to Lumina

A calm space for stronger movement

Lumina Physiotherapy is a modern rehabilitation clinic dedicated to helping you overcome pain, restore function, and return to the activities you love. Whether you’re recovering from injury, managing a chronic condition, or optimizing athletic performance, we meet you where you are.

Our approach blends hands-on therapy, progressive exercise, and clear education so you understand your body and stay active for the long term.
