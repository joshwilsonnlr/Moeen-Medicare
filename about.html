About Our Physiotherapy Care

Dedicated to helping patients in Bahawalpur move better and live with less pain.

Our Clinic

Moeen Medicare and Infertility Hospital

Located in Satellite Town, Bahawalpur, Moeen Medicare and Infertility Hospital provides healthcare services to the local community. Our physiotherapy department, led by Dr. Ramsha Ramzan, focuses on rehabilitation, pain management, and restoring physical function.

We believe that effective physiotherapy starts with listening carefully to each patient and building a treatment plan that fits their goals, lifestyle, and condition.

Our Approach

How We Care for Patients

1

Patient-Centered

Every treatment plan is built around the individual — their symptoms, goals, and daily life demands.

2

Evidence-Based

We use proven physiotherapy techniques and current clinical knowledge to guide assessment and treatment.

3

Education & Empowerment

We help you understand your condition and give you tools and exercises to support recovery at home.

Our Values

What Guides Our Care

  • Clear communication and honest guidance
  • Respect for every patient’s time and concerns
  • Focus on functional recovery, not just symptoms
  • Continuous learning and professional standards
