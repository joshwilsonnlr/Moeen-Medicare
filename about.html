About Lumina

Care that feels personal

We believe physiotherapy should be clear, collaborative, and focused on real progress — not just temporary relief.

Our Story

A clinic built around better movement

Lumina Physiotherapy was founded to provide thoughtful, high-quality rehabilitation in a calm and modern environment. We combine clinical expertise with genuine attention so every patient feels heard and supported.

Our mission is simple: help people move better, recover with confidence, and stay active in the activities that matter to them — whether that means returning to sport, managing daily pain, or rebuilding after surgery.

How We Work

Our approach to patient care

Every plan is shaped by assessment, evidence, and your individual goals.

Thorough Assessment

Personalized Care

Treatment combines hands-on therapy, progressive exercise, and practical strategies tailored to your life.

Sustainable Progress

We focus on lasting change — helping you build strength, awareness, and independence beyond the clinic.
