Meet Our Physiotherapist

Professional, patient-focused care in Bahawalpur.

Dr. Ramsha Ramzan

Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT) · NeuroMusculoSkeletal Expert

Dr. Ramsha Ramzan is a physiotherapist and rehabilitation specialist dedicated to helping patients recover from injury, manage pain, and improve movement. Her approach is practical, patient-centered, and built around clear assessment and individualized care plans.

She combines thorough assessment, hands-on treatment, and well-structured exercise programs so patients understand their condition and can actively participate in recovery.
